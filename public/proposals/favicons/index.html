Favicon proposal / 01

One tiny tab.
Five directions.

The brief

A little record-sleeve personality for Bill’s corner of the internet.

Keep the dark palette and coral. Make it recognizable at 16 pixels. These are five original SVG drawings, not five variations on the same play button.

A point of view

Keep Bill
in the picture.

I’d start with 01 / Blackletter B. It belongs to the visual world already on the site, and makes the icon about the person behind the playlists—not the plumbing underneath.

For a more playful direction, 03 / Record flower is my runner-up. It feels like something you’d find on a record-shop tote.

After you pick one

  1. 01
    Polish the small version.

    Refine the chosen silhouette at 16 and 32 pixels, not just at poster size.

  2. 02
    Make the little icon family.

    Prepare the final SVG, raster favicon fallbacks, and a matching touch icon.

  3. 03
    Wire it up and ship.

    Add the approved assets to the app, check light and dark tabs, and deploy. No new service or dependency.

Original drawings. Portable files.

All five marks were drawn for this proposal. No Noun Project or other third-party icon assets were used, so there’s no borrowed-icon attribution or license to manage. Each SVG is self-contained: paths and shapes, no fonts, scripts, or remote assets.

This is a design recommendation, not a trademark clearance. The existing wordmark font appears only in this page’s header, not in the SVGs.
